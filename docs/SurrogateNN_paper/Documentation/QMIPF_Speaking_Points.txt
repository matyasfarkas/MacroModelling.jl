SLIDE 1: WHAT IS THIS PROJECT? (45 seconds)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Replicated the Quantitative Microfounded IPF Model (Adrian et al. 2021) in Julia
- Original: 180+ equations in Dynare/Matlab
- New: Full implementation in MacroModelling.jl package
- WHY: Enable nonlinear solution methods not available in Dynare
  * Stochastic Extended Path (SEP) algorithm
  * Mixed Complementarity Problems for occasionally binding constraints

KEY MESSAGE: "We now have a Julia-native QMIPF that matches Dynare AND goes beyond it"


SLIDE 2: WHAT DOES THE MODEL CAPTURE? (45 seconds)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Core Structure (200 variables, 120 shocks, 119 parameters):
- Small open economy with nominal rigidities (Calvo prices + wages)
- CES aggregation of domestic and imported goods
- Real UIP with endogenous risk premium (Gabaix-Maggiori)
- Taylor rule monetary policy

IPF Policy Tools:
1. Conventional monetary policy (interest rate)
2. FX intervention (TAU_F wedge in UIP)
3. Capital flow management (debt limits, risk premium)

KEY MESSAGE: "Full microfoundations for analyzing EME policy tradeoffs"


SLIDE 3: VALIDATION - JULIA MATCHES DYNARE (45 seconds)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Three-way comparison for 1-sigma shocks:
1. Dynare first-order perturbation (reference)
2. Julia deterministic SEP (order=0)
3. Julia stochastic SEP (order=20)

Results (see Figures 1-3):
- Perfect alignment at 1-sigma: validates implementation
- All three methods yield identical IRFs for small shocks
- Difference < 0.1% for output, consumption, investment

KEY MESSAGE: "The Julia implementation is validated against Dynare"


SLIDE 4: THE NOVELTY - IMPACT OF FUTURE UNCERTAINTY (1 minute)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

What stochastic SEP captures that linear methods miss:
- Agents integrate over FUTURE shock realizations
- Uses Gauss-Hermite quadrature (3 nodes per shock)
- Order=20 means looking 20 periods ahead with uncertainty

Key findings at 2-3 sigma shocks (see Figures 4-12):
1. Linear scaling breaks down
2. Stochastic SEP shows 4-9% DAMPENING vs deterministic
3. Precautionary behavior emerges under uncertainty
4. Financial variables (Q, NFA) diverge more than real variables

Exception: NFA can be AMPLIFIED (+0.6-0.7%) due to portfolio rebalancing

KEY MESSAGE: "Future uncertainty matters quantitatively for policy analysis"

================================================================================
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
================================================================================

Q: Why Julia instead of Dynare?
A: SEP algorithm implementation + future estimation/optimal policy extensions

Q: Why does uncertainty dampen responses?
A: Precautionary motive - agents save more, invest less when future is uncertain

Q: Why is NFA amplified not dampened?
A: Portfolio rebalancing - uncertainty triggers larger external adjustments

Q: What's the computational cost?
A: Stochastic SEP is 100x slower than linear, but still practical (minutes not hours)

Q: Can we do estimation?
A: Not yet, but MacroModelling.jl infrastructure supports it - planned extension
